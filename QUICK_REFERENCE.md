## 🎮 How to Use (2 minutes)

1. **See the word** - Displayed at the top
2. **Hear pronunciation** - Click 🔊 button
3. **Read definition** - Shows word meaning
4. **Type spelling** - In the input box
5. **Press Enter** - To submit
6. **See feedback** - ✅ or ❌
7. **Progress updates** - Bar fills up
8. **Next word** - Appears automatically

## 🎓 Understanding the Smart Reordering

The app learns from your practice:

**You spell word correctly** → Word goes to end (mastered)  
**You spell word wrong** → Word stays at front (needs practice)  
**Never tried word** → Word appears first (new)  

**Result**: Perfect adaptive learning! 🎯
